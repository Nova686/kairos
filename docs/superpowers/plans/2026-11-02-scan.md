# Scan Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On an iPhone at home, add a product by scanning its barcode, a PSA certificate, or a photo of the box; Kairos proposes the product directly when it is sure, otherwise a few candidates, and learns from every confirmation.

**Architecture:** Barcode decoding runs in the browser (`@zxing/browser`, Safari has no `BarcodeDetector`). Identification runs on the server in a fixed chain (shared catalogue → Open Beauty Facts → UPCitemdb), reusing plan 1's lookups and plan 2's candidate search. Photo recognition runs entirely on the server, free: OCR with `tesseract.js` and image embeddings with a local CLIP model through `@huggingface/transformers`, stored in Postgres with `pgvector` (PGlite's `vector` extension in tests and local dev). One pure scoring function merges both signals.

**Tech Stack:** Plan 2's stack, plus `@zxing/browser` 0.2, `tesseract.js` 7, `@huggingface/transformers` 4 (model `Xenova/clip-vit-base-patch32`, 512 dimensions), pgvector.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-kairos-design.md` (objective 2, section 5 « iPhone / Safari », section 7 « Code-barres », « Cartes gradées », « Reconnaissance photo »). Depends on plans 1 and 2; uses plan 3's refresh queue when present (optional).

## Global Constraints

- Plan 2's Global Constraints apply unchanged.
- Nothing paid; models and OCR language data are downloaded once at first use into `.data/models` and `.data/tesseract` (configurable through `NUXT_MODELS_DIRECTORY`), never fetched on every request.
- Photos are not kept: only their embedding is stored once the user confirms the product. Uploads are capped at 5 MB, resized client-side to at most 1024 px on the longest side, JPEG quality 0.85.
- Camera needs HTTPS: in local dev, the camera works on `localhost`; on the phone, only on the deployed site (plan 5).
- `KAIROS_PHOTO_RECOGNITION=off` disables the photo path (small fallback server, plan 5); the UI then hides the photo button and says why.
- Auto-pick threshold: top score ≥ 0.85 **and** at least 0.10 above the second; otherwise show the top 5.

## Review Focus

- A barcode already linked to a product never calls Open Beauty Facts or UPCitemdb.
- UPCitemdb answering 429 (daily limit) degrades to « code inconnu, cherchez par texte », the barcode is still offered for linking after the text search.
- Camera permission denied or no camera: the scan page shows the manual barcode field and the photo upload (file input with `capture="environment"`) instead of a blank video.
- A photo of a product with no image in the catalogue yet still returns OCR-based candidates.
- Confirming a scanned product twice does not store a duplicate barcode link nor a duplicate embedding for the same upload.

### Task 5: OCR and candidate scoring

**Files:**
- Create: `server/domain/scan/box-text-reader.ts`, `shared/scan/extract-search-words.ts`, `shared/scan/score-photo-candidates.ts`
- Test: `tests/unit/scan/extract-search-words.test.ts`, `tests/unit/scan/score-photo-candidates.test.ts`, `tests/slow/scan/box-text-reader.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `type BoxTextReader = { read(image: Buffer): Promise<string> }` — `createTesseractBoxTextReader(languageDirectory)`, languages `fra+eng`, one worker reused.
  - `extractSearchWords(ocrText: string): string[]` — lower-case, accents kept, words of 3+ letters, stop words removed (`pokemon`, `pokémon`, `the`, `les`, `des`, `jeu`, `cartes`, `card`, `game`, `company`, `nintendo`, `ans`, `age`), de-duplicated, at most 8, longest first.
  - `type PhotoCandidateSignal = { productId: string | null; candidateKey: string; label: string; visualSimilarity: number | null; candidateName: string }`
  - `scorePhotoCandidates(signals: PhotoCandidateSignal[], searchWords: string[]): { candidateKey: string; score: number }[]` — `textScore` = share of the candidate name's words (3+ letters, accent-insensitive) found among `searchWords`; `score = visualSimilarity === null ? 0.7 × textScore : 0.6 × visualSimilarity + 0.4 × textScore`; sorted descending.
  - `shouldAutoPick(scores): boolean` — Global Constraints threshold.
